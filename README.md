### My Favorite Movie

**The God Father**

- It's about mafia families plotting against each other for control and power.
- The main character is a soldier and son of the most powerful person in their city. 
- His older sibling dies after an ambush, and then his father was hospitalized also due to an ambush but later died because of complications.
- Then he kills the head of the other families that plot against his, and became the head of his organization and ruled the city.
